What the house does

Wara works in four families. Gourmand fragrances read sweet and edible, built on nuts, cream and tonka. Fruity florals open bright and dry down to woods. Aquatics stay clean and citrus-led. Oriental woody scents carry spice, resin and leather into the evening.

Each fragrance is sold under a stated price, with its note pyramid published on the product page. Nothing in the range is hidden behind a price on request.

Wearing a Wara fragrance

Four habits get more out of the bottle, whichever family you choose.

  1. Spray on moisturised skin

    Fragrance holds longer on skin that has just been moisturised, than on skin that is dry.

  2. Keep it to pulse points

    Wrists, the base of the throat, behind the ears. Warm skin releases the scent slowly.

  3. Do not rub it in

    Rubbing breaks the top notes and shortens the wear. Let it settle where it lands.

  4. Store it out of the light

    Heat and daylight flatten a fragrance. A closed drawer at room temperature is enough.
