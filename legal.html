Terms, privacy and delivery

These pages set out how ordering, privacy and delivery work at Wara Fragrance. They are written in plain language so you can read them once and know where you stand.

This is a plain-language summary of the house's practice, not legal advice. Have it reviewed before you rely on it.

Terms of service

Who you are ordering from

Wara Fragrance is a perfume house based in Mombasa, Kenya. You can reach the house on WhatsApp at 0746 410 595.

Orders

  • An order is a request until the house confirms it. Confirmation happens in the WhatsApp conversation.
  • Prices are listed in US dollars. The house confirms the price, the delivery cost and the payment method with you before dispatch.
  • If an item is out of stock, the house will tell you and offer the nearest alternative or a refund of anything already paid.

Your bag on this website

The bag on this site is stored in your own browser. It is a record of what you intend to order, not a completed purchase, and it is not sent anywhere until you choose to send the order on WhatsApp.

Accuracy

Fragrance notes and descriptions are reproduced from the house's own product listings. Scent is experienced differently by different people, so a note list is a guide rather than a promise.

Liability

Wara Fragrance is responsible for getting your order to you in good condition. Nothing here limits any right you have under Kenyan consumer law.

Privacy policy

What this website collects

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not set advertising cookies. Your bag is kept in your own browser's local storage and stays there.

What the house collects when you order

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address, because a courier needs them.
  • What you ordered, so the order can be packed and, if needed, refunded.

That information is collected in the WhatsApp conversation, not on this website.

What we do with it

  • We use it to fulfil your order.
  • We do not sell it, and we do not share it beyond what delivery requires.
  • We keep order records only as long as we need them for accounting and returns.

Your choices

Ask the house on WhatsApp to show you what is held about you, correct it, or delete it. Requests are answered on the same line you ordered from.

Delivery and returns

Delivery

  • The house delivers anywhere in Kenya. Delivery cost and timing are confirmed with you before dispatch.
  • Gift packs can be boxed and sent straight to the recipient if you say so when you order.

Returns

  • Tell the house within seven days of delivery if something arrived damaged, wrong, or not what you ordered.
  • Sealed, unopened bottles can be returned. Opened fragrance cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, which is standard for cosmetics.
  • Refunds are made through the same method the payment came in on.

Something missing?

If your question is not answered here, ask on WhatsApp at 0746 410 595, and a person will answer.
