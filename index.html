How to wear it well

Perfume behaves differently on every person. These four habits get more out of the bottle.

  1. Spray on moisturised skin

    Fragrance holds longer on skin that has just been moisturised, than on skin that is dry.

  2. Keep it to pulse points

    Wrists, the base of the throat, behind the ears. Warm skin releases the scent slowly.

  3. Do not rub it in

    Rubbing breaks the top notes and shortens the wear. Let it settle where it lands.

  4. Store it out of the light

    Heat and daylight flatten a fragrance. A closed drawer at room temperature is enough.
